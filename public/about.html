Short Links for Everyone

ProLinks turns public file URLs into short download links you can share — fast, in the browser, with no app and no account.

Our Mission

Simplify Access to Public Files

Many workflows require a concise, shareable link to a file that already exists at a public URL. ProLinks provides a short download URL you can copy, send, and open on any device—without installing software or creating an account.

The process is straightforward: submit the source address, receive a ProLinks link, then download or share as needed. We support common formats across video, images, documents, audio, and archives, prioritizing accurate retrieval of the original file when the source allows it.

We do not host your content. The service applies only to public URLs—not password-protected or authenticated resources. Short links are short-lived and are removed automatically after 24 hours. You are responsible for using ProLinks only with material you are permitted to access and share, in accordance with our Terms of Use and DMCA policy.
