Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's exactly how we handle your data — spoiler: we barely collect any.

Last updated: January 1, 2025

🔒 Short version: We don't store your download URLs, we don't track you, and we don't sell your data. ProLinks is built with privacy-first principles.

📦 Data We Collect

We collect the bare minimum necessary to provide our service:

  • Basic browser and device type (for compatibility)
  • Country-level location (for legal compliance, never precise)
  • Anonymous usage statistics (page views, feature usage)
  • If you create an account: email address and username

We do not log or store the URLs you paste into ProLinks. Download requests are processed in memory only.

🎯 How We Use Data

The limited data we collect is used only to:

  • Improve service performance and reliability
  • Detect and prevent abuse or fraudulent activity
  • Send important account notifications (if you sign up)
  • Comply with legal obligations

🍪 Cookies

We use minimal, essential cookies only:

  • Session cookies (temporary, deleted on browser close)
  • Preference cookies (e.g., your selected download quality)

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that identify you personally.

🔗 Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data only in these limited cases:

  • To comply with a legal obligation or court order
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users
  • With service providers who help operate our platform (under strict agreements)

✅ Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of any communications from us

To exercise these rights, contact us at prolinks@proton.me

🛡️ Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption, regular security audits, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure — we encourage safe browsing habits.

👶 Children's Privacy

ProLinks is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us immediately.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email. Continued use of ProLinks after changes constitutes acceptance.

📧 Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at:

  • Email: prolinks@proton.me
  • Response time: within 48 hours
