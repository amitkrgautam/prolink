Can I share the link with others?+
Yes! Generated short links are stored on our server, so anyone with the link can open it on any device. Share freely with friends, family, or colleagues.
Is it legal to download files using ProLinks?+
Downloading files for personal use is generally acceptable. However, redistributing copyrighted content without permission may violate terms of service or copyright laws. Always respect the original creator's rights.
Does ProLinks work on private or locked links?+
ProLinks can only download from public URLs. Private files, password-protected pages, or links requiring authentication are not supported at this time.

We support virtually all common file formats — MP4, MKV, MOV, JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF, DOCX, XLSX, MP3, ZIP, RAR and many more. We always fetch the original format.
Is ProLinks safe? Do you store my data?+
Yes, ProLinks is completely safe. Links are stored on our server only to allow sharing. We do not store personal data or install anything on your device.
